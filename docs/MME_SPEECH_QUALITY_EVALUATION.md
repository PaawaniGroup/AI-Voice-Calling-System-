## 2. Evaluation Sheet & Rubric Guidelines

### Evaluation Scale (1 to 5):
- **Intelligibility (1–5):** 1 = Unintelligible; 3 = Understandable with effort; 5 = Crystal clear, every word distinct.
- **Naturalness (1–5):** 1 = Highly robotic / robotic cadence; 3 = Moderate robotic coloring; 5 = Human-like speech cadence.
- **Pronunciation (1–5):** 1 = Severe phonetic errors; 3 = Minor accent deviations; 5 = Bit-perfect Indian English / Hindi phonetics.
- **Boundary Artifacts (1–5):** 1 = Loud clicks, pops, or stutter at chunk seams; 3 = Noticeable phase jumps; 5 = Seamless transitions.
